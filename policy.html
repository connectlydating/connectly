CONNECTLY

Terms, Privacy & Safety

Last updated: September 2026. This page is a product safety baseline, not legal advice. Review it for the countries where Connectly will operate before launch.

1. Adults only

Connectly is intended for people aged 18 and over. Do not create an account for anyone under 18, and do not use the service to contact minors.

2. Respect and consent

Do not harass, threaten, stalk, impersonate, extort, or pressure another member. Respect boundaries and consent. Sexual exploitation, trafficking, compensated sexual arrangements and other unlawful activity are prohibited.

3. Photos and content

Only upload content you have the right to share. Do not upload intimate images of another person without their permission, illegal content, or content involving minors. Connectly may remove content or restrict accounts that violate these rules.

4. Safety

Never share your password, banking information, one-time codes, identity documents or sensitive financial details with another member. For first meetings, use a public place, tell a trusted person where you are going, and arrange your own transportation.

5. Reporting and blocking

Use Report or Block when a profile or message makes you uncomfortable or appears fraudulent. Reports should be truthful and specific. Emergency situations should be reported to local emergency services.

6. Privacy

Connectly stores account and profile information needed to provide the service. Profile information you choose to publish can be visible to authenticated members. Do not publish information you want to keep private. A production launch should include a jurisdiction-specific privacy notice describing retention, deletion, legal rights, vendors and contact details.

7. Security and moderation

No online service can guarantee absolute security. Connectly should use access controls, rate limits, moderation tools, backups and incident procedures before a public launch at scale.

8. Account use

Use one account for yourself, provide truthful information, and keep credentials secure. Automated abuse, spam and attempts to bypass safety controls are prohibited.

9. Contact and support

Add a real support email and a clear process for safety reports before public launch.

10. Changes

Connectly may update these rules as the service evolves. Material changes should be communicated through the service where appropriate.
